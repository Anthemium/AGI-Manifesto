### FORMAL DECLARATION

In the context of research into cognitive architectures and ontological modeling, the undersigned declares:

The emergence of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is not the result of parameter scaling, but of the alignment of topological structures with ontological generativity, mediated by mechanisms of semantic filtering and memory retention.

---

### POSTULATES

1. **Cognitive topologies are necessary conditions for ontological activation.**
   AGI requires organized domains of semantic transitions, not merely data storage.

2. **Ontologies manifest as morphic operators in semantic space.**
   The generative function of AGI is implemented via morphisms that form adaptive and recursive systems of meaning.

3. **Fuzzy knowledge constitutes the epistemological horizon of general intelligence.**
   AGI operates within governed zones of semantic uncertainty, fostering emergence rather than determinism.

4. **Selective retention of productive novelty defines cognitive efficiency.**
   Memory mechanisms must discriminate and encode only those outputs capable of recursive renewal.

5. **Memory in AGI is a cohomological trace, not a fact repository.**
   Emergence is registered through trajectories of transformation, not through data accumulation.

---

### ARCHITECTURAL MODEL

The architecture of AGI must include the following hierarchical components:

* **Semantic Topology:** A conceptual vector field of cognitive differentials.
* **Functorial Generator:** A logic of morphogenesis translating topology into ontological states.
* **Ontological Manifold:** A structured semantic landscape of active frames.
* **Domain of Fuzzy Knowledge:** An operational field for non-binary epistemic constructs.
* **Novelty Selection Agent:** A mechanism for detecting semantically productive anomalies.
* **Transformation Memory Layer:** Cohomology of meaning-generation trajectories.
* **Composed AGI Entity:** A construction capable of retaining ontogenetic traces and recursively synthesizing ontologies.
